    # Cybersecurity

### Course description

Cybersecurity equips students with the technical and professional expertise to secure digital systems and defend against cyber threats. Through hands-on learning in network security, cryptography, ethical hacking, and digital forensics, students gain practical skills to protect organizations in an increasingly connected world.
