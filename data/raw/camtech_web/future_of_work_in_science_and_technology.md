Science and Technology

CamTech will host a Webinar on Future of Work in Science and Technology on 11th September 2021 from 9:00 – 11:30 am. The purposes are to discuss the latest trends and new dimensions in science and technology. This webinar is open to the public.

Co-organized : National Institute of Science, Technology and Innovation (NISTI), Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation (MISTI)

Agendas:

- The importance of student internship
- New dimensions of science and technology
- Panel discussion:
  - What are the emerging demands in the field of science and technology?
  - How is an internship important to students for job readiness?
  - What are the expectations of the industries to graduated students?
