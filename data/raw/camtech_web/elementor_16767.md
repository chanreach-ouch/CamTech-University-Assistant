British Ambassador Mr. Dominic Williams visited CamTech University

Welcome H.E. Ambassador Mr. Dominic Williams, British High Commission, Cambodia to CamTech University on Wednesday the 12th of October, 2022.
During the visit, several discussions took place about opportunity, collaboration, and mutual support between CamTech and the British Embassy like research on science and technology, staff and student exchange programs, scholarships, and the Applied Mathematics Center in helping students with mathematics.
