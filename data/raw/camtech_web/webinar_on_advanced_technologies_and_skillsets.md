CamTech will host a **Webinar on Advanced Technologies and Skillsets** on 18th September 2021 from 9:00 – 10:30 am. The purposes of the webinar are to discuss the roles of science, technology and innovation in economic development and innovation and entrepreneurship opportunities for students. This webinar is open to the public. 

Co-organized : National Institute of Science, Technology and Innovation (NISTI), Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation (MISTI)

**Agendas:**

- Roles of science, technology and innovation in economic development
- Innovation and entrepreneurship opportunities for students
- Panel discussion:
  - The Importance of Science and Technology
  - What opportunities do you think should be provided by university to encourage students to involve in Innovation and Entrepreneurship?
  - What are the essential skills that students or fresh graduates need in order to establish their startups?
  - What are the challenges that you have faced when you began your startup? How did you deal with those obstacles?
