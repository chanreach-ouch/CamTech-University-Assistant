Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech) is an upcoming **world class higher education institution**.

CamTech’s core values are **Integrity**, **Respect**, **Excellence**, **Innovation**, **Diversity** which we call *IREID*.

Our motto is **Knowledge**, **Reason**, **Character** and we proudly believe to be able to make a difference in the existing education environment in Cambodia. We work on our academic programs aiming to bring **innovation** and **knowledge** that will contribute to the **economic** and **social progress** for the existing and the future generations.
