Congrats to CamTech University and Concordia University (Canada) on signing a groundbreaking Higher Education MoU!

Congratulations to CamTech University and Concordia University (Canada) on the official signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) for a groundbreaking Higher Education Collaboration!

This MoU sets the stage for a stronger academic partnership, with key areas of collaboration, including:

– Joint Research Projects

– Faculty & Student Exchange Programs

– 3+1 Undergraduate Pathways

– Graduate Scholarship Opportunities

We are excited for the opportunities this partnership will bring, enhancing educational pathways and fostering global learning experiences for students and faculty. Here’s to building a brighter academic future together!
