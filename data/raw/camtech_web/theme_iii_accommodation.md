In collaboration with the Canadia Group, CamTech University offers modern and comfortable condominium accommodations for students. These condos provide a safe and welcoming environment, complete with essential amenities, ensuring a pleasant and convenient stay throughout your studies. The condos are conveniently located near the campus, and students can benefit from a special corporate rate, with additional subsidies provided by the university to make it more affordable.
