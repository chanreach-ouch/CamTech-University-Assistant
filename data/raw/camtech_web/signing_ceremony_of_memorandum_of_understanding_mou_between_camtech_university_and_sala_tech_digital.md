Signing Ceremony of Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between CamTech University and Sala Tech Digital Solution (Cambodia) Co., Ltd

On November 2, 2022, CamTech University signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Sala Tech Digital Solution (Cambodia). The event was held at the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports with the participation of a number of universities and institutes. The purpose of this signing ceremony is to provide an opportunity for students in the provinces to easily find more information about their majors in higher education and higher education institutions, to promote higher education, especially in technology, and meet the needs of the labor market, and to promote young people in the field of Stem in Cambodia. The signing ceremony was fully supported by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports.
