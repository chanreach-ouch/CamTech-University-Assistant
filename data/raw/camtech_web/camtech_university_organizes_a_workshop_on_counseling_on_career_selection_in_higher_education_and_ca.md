On the afternoon of June 23, 2022, CamTech University organized a workshop on counseling on career selection in higher education and career orientation for the future at Samdech Akka Moha Thamma Pothisal Chea Sim Prek Anchanh High School. The workshop was attended by a total of 100 teachers and students. The purpose of this workshop was to share knowledge on major selection in higher education, especially related to STEM skills, which are in high demand in the current and future job market; and to encourage students to make the right decisions as well as to get prepared for university. In addition, CamTech students also shared some of their experiences of studying at CamTech University and presented various projects, such as VR / AR Games, to students at Chea Sim Prek Anchanh High School. CamTech University would like to thank the principal and all the teachers for their support and facilitation of the workshop.
