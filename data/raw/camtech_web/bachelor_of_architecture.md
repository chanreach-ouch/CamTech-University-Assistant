## Architecture

## Program Overview

Architecture has been defined diversely throughout history, and its practice has always been shaped by the advancement of innovative technology. Technology, particularly Artificial Intelligence (AI), has been dominating every aspect of our built environment. CamTech’s Architecture Program is designed to equip students with skills to use technology ethically, and the core of it is the smart Design Studio which encourages students to have an in-depth understanding of building structures and critical issues in architectural design. Students will be able to immerse themselves in new cutting-edge technology in construction and produce more sustainable buildings locally and internationally. We empower students to use new digital architecture technology such as AI, data analysis, 3D modelling for design, virtual reality, and so on to enable optimal design, sustainable architecture, energy efficient safe environment, and time-saving. CamTech offers a work-integrated learning experience to develop students’ employability, entrepreneurial, and leadership skills.

## Program Structure

The 4-year Bachelor’s Degree program contains 156 credits combining from 126 credits of coursework, 18 credits of Virtubiz and 12 credits of Industrial Placement and Graduation Project.
