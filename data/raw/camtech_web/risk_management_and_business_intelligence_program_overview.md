# Risk Management and Business Intelligence Program Overview

December 6, 2023 2025-11-17 13:57
## Program Overview

A Bachelor’s degree in Risk Management and Business Intelligence is a program that provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the intersection between business analytics, information technology, and risk management. The study combines theoretical concepts with practical applications in order to prepare students to analyse data and make informed decisions that mitigate business risks and improve organizational performance.

Students learn to develop risk management strategies, assess potential threats to businesses, evaluate financial risks, and analyse market trends. They also gain skills in data mining, statistical analysis, decision-making, database management, and software development. The program covers topics such as business intelligence, strategic management, enterprise risk management, fraud detection and prevention, cyber security, and compliance. Graduates can pursue careers in various industries, including finance, insurance, healthcare, government, and consulting firms.

Overall, a Bachelor’s degree in Risk Management and Business Intelligence provides students with a strong foundation in both technical and business skills, making them well-equipped to succeed in today’s data-driven business world.

## Program Learning Outcomes

At the end of the 4-year program, graduates will be able to:

- Use quantitative and qualitative tools to assess risk and identify potential threats to businesses and organizations.
- Develop and implement comprehensive risk management plans to minimize losses and protect the interests of their companies and clients.
- Apply critical thinking and analytical skills to business intelligence data and use it to recommend data-driven strategies to help organizations remain competitive.
- Demonstrate knowledge of information security principles to protect data privacy and prevent data breaches.
- Effectively communicate complex risk management, business intelligence, and compliance issues to stakeholders, including senior executives, regulators, employees, and clients.

## Program Structure

The 4-year Bachelor’s Degree program contains 156 credits combining from 106 credits of coursework, 48 credits of internship and 2 credits of Industrial Placement Report. The courses are divided into three categories, including Common Courses, Specialized Courses, and Elective Courses.
