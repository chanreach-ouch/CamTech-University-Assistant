Phnom Penh, October 25, 2024 – The Buddhist AI Ethics Seminar Series 3, titled “Buddhism and AI: Reality, Ethics, and Governance,” convened a diverse group of participants and esteemed speakers to explore the intricate relationships between artificial intelligence (AI) and Buddhist ethical frameworks. This seminar has served as a vital platform for reflection and dialogue on how these two domains can inform one another as we navigate the complexities of modern technology.
