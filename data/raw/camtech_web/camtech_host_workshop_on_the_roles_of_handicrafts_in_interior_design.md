CamTech host workshop on "The Roles of Handicrafts in Interior Design"

Have you ever thought of beginning your own startup? And have you ever had a business idea but don’t know how to make it come true? These questions will be addressed in a workshop to be hosted at CamTech on “The Roles of Handicrafts in Interior Design”, in which Ms. Ry Thany, a specialist in interior decor for hotels and resorts, is our key speaker.
