CamTech family consistently shows support for sports! On Saturday, July 22, 2023, both students and members of the CamTech family participated in the PPCC Fun Run and KKCK Elite Race, which included 5km and 10km distances.

This event is one of many activities organized alongside our sports scheduled at CamTech University. CamTech believes that engaging in sports not only brings enjoyment but also contributes to maintaining good health.

CamTech consistently encourages and supports our students and staff to take part in sport activities to keep-up CamTech sport spirit.
