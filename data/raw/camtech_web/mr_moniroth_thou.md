## Mr. Moniroth Thou

###### Mr. Thou Moniroth

Marketing and Sales Officer

Mr. Moniroth Thou holds a Bachelor of Fine and Applied Arts in Industrial Design from the School of Architecture and Design at King Mongkut’s University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT). During his journey, he gained international exposure through several design workshops and programs, including the Asia Design Global Workshop in Tokyo, the Asia-Link Bangkok Workshop in Bangkok, and a Sustainability & SDGs program in Taiwan. With a strong passion for innovation and a business-driven mindset, Mr. Moniroth applies his expertise in design and research to transform ideas into practical opportunities. At CamTech University, he contributes to the university’s growth by supporting the development of strategic campaigns and initiatives that enhance institutional visibility and engagement. He is detail-oriented, continuously pushes boundaries, and believes that “luck is hackable.”
