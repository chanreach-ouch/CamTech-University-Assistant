On November 14, 2022, a group of CamTech students led by Ms. Noy Chhorvin, Student Affairs Specialist, paid visit to ISI GROUP . We are proud to partner with the ISI GROUP. ISI GROUP offers scholarships to outstanding students studying at CamTech University. The ISI Group shares the current Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) activity implementation by the KHM Foundation. Part of the site visit, Architects from Urbanland shared a BROWN COFFEE design concept presentation to students who are majoring in Architecture and Interior design.
