On Sunday morning, 01 May 2022, CamTech students and staff, led by Dr. Khieng Sothy, joined a charity fun run organized by Indochina Star Fish (ISF) Cambodia on Koh Dach Island. This shows the spirit of charity and sport always championed by CamTech as part of the university culture.
