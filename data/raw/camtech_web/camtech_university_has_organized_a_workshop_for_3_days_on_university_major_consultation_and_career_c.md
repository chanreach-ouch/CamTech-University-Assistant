CamTech University has organized a workshop for 3 days on "University Major Consultation and Career Counseling” for the students at Bun Rany Hun Sen Memut High School, Tbong Khmum Province, Khov Bunsun High School, Queen Kosomak High School, and Seila Veti Keat Chhun High School at Kratie Province

To help students choose the right majors and to provide a positive impact on their future career choices, CamTech University has organized a workshop for 3 days on “University Major Consultation and Career Counseling” for the students who are studying in 12th grade at Bun Rany Hun Sen Memut High School, Tbong Khmum Province, Khov Bunsun High School, Kratie City, Queen Kosomak High School, and Seila Veti Keat Chhun High School at Kratie Province. About 534 teachers and students participated. In this workshop, CamTech students also shared their university study experience and their internship projects.

CamTech is always ready to provide free counseling on STEM majors and STEM-related careers to high school and university students.
