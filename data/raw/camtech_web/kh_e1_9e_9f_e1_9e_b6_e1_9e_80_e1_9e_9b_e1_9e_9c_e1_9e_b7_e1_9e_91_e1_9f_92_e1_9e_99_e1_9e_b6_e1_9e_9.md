CamTech University organizes a workshop on “The roles of handicrafts in interior design”

To link lessons learned from classes to actual practices in the workplace, in the morning of 30 April 2022, CamTech University organized a workshop on “The roles of handicrafts in interior design”. Invited to speak in this workshop was Ms. Ry Thany of Norea Angkor Handicrafts. The purposes of this workshop were:

1. To understand how to begin a startup in Interior Design

2. To gain an insight into the characteristics and values of startups

3. To get to know the roles of local handicrafts in Interior Design and to promote tourism
