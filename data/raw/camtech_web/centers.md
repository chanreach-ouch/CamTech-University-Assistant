As a research-intensive university, research and innovation have been a core mission of CamTech because they are the key essence to ensure scientific advancements and Cambodia’s industrial development and socio-economic growth. In addition, through their high-level research and new discoveries, CamTech’s academics can generate new knowledge and deliver research-led teaching. Owning to its direct and indirect advantages, since the university establishment, research and innovation centers have been founded to foster excellence in both pure and applied research, academic education, services, and scholarly collaboration.

Currently, CamTech has eight research centers.
