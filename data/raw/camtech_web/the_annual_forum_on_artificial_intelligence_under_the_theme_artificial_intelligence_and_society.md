The Annual Forum on Artificial Intelligence under the theme “Artificial Intelligence and Society

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has opened a new chapter to socio-economic growth, enhances efficiency, lowers production costs, and addresses various challenges. But questions have also been raised about the possible negative impacts of AI on society. And unfortunately, no single actor has all the capacity to offer the answers let alone potential solutions. Therefore, it is necessary to act now and act together to provide responses and direct the development and use of AI for the greater good.

The Annual Forum on Artificial Intelligence under the theme “Artificial Intelligence and Society” will investigate the AI landscape in Cambodia and its influence on society as a whole and highlights key policy questions for promoting and strengthening the development of Industry 4.0 technologies in general, AI in particular.
