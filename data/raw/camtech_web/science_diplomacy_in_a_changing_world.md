CamTech University, in collaboration with the National Institute of Science, Technology and Innovation (NISTI) and the AI Forum, co-organized the workshop “Science Diplomacy in a Changing World” on 29 April 2025

This important event brought together diplomats, academics, scientists, and industry leaders to explore how science diplomacy can advance international cooperation, respond to global challenges, and ensure inclusive access to emerging technologies. Discussions emphasized the vital role of linking research to policy and fostering cross-border scientific collaboration to drive innovation, inform decision-making, and promote sustainable development.
