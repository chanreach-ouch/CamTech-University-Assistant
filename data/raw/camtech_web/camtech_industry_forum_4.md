The Industry Forum & Career Fair 2025 under the theme “**Education for Transformation**” aims to strengthen collaboration between CamTech University and key industry partners. This event will serve as a platform where students can explore career pathways, meet potential employers, and gain insights into emerging industry trends,

while companies can identify and engage with talented young professionals for internship and junior recruitment opportunities
