The Director of Techo Startup Center led a delegate team to visit the Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech)

In the morning of 06 May 2022, H.E. Dr. Taing Nguonly, executive director of Techo Startup Center, led a delegate team to visit the Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech) to discuss a possible collaboration. The discussion underscored digital skills, which are necessary in the 21st century, the possibility of sending CamTech students to various competitions, and the possible collaboration with Techo Startup Center. At the same time, the two parties also discussed human resource exchange between the two institutions in a form called “talent pool” on research, business, and technology. After the discussion, the delegation went around CamTech campus to see our facilities, labs, and three project rooms. CamTech is currently running three major projects: an AI-powered motorbike training using VR App, an AI-powered plant diagnosis and optimal fertilizer spreading model using drone & hyperspectral camera, and interior design using augmented and mixed reality.
