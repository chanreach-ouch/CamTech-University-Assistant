On Monday, 21<sup>st</sup> March 2022, the students of CamTech University were given a captivating and essential special lecture by Professor Chhem Kieth Rethy on the topic “A Life in Science”.

Explaining the differences between nature, nurture, and chance has enlightened young people as they realize that humans are all born with unique abilities. As a result, it is vitally important to maintain our self-confidence and positive attitudes to keep moving forward in the face of challenges.

Professor Rethy’s speech about his life journey to becoming a scientist has inspired all the CamTech students to pursue their dreams and do what they believe is right for themselves. “I have no regrets in my life because I do what I want to do,” the professor responded to one of the students’ inquiry. These remarks have enabled the students to perceive their own potential.

As long as you hold an unwavering passion or potential for a particular endeavor, nothing is impossible in life.
