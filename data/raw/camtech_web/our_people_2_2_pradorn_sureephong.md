Visiting Professor

Asst. Prof. Pradorn Sureephong, Ph.D. received his double degree in Informatique from Universite Lumiere Lyon 2, France and in Knowledge Management from Chiang Mai University, Thailand. He is the associate director in innovation and joint ventures and also head of master degree program in Digital Technology Management at College of Arts, Media and Technology. He is also the president of the Electrical, Electronics, Computer and Telecommunications Engineering Association (ECTI – North Section). He secured multiple research grants from British Council (2012), French Embassy – PHC SIAM (2015), Horizon H2020 by European Commission (2017). He is now the local coordinator of 3 Erasmus+ programme i.e. SUNSpACe (Sustainable Development Smart Agriculture Capacity, CENTRAL (Digital Transformation Capacity Building), DigiHealth (Digital Health and Monitoring). He is also one of the leaders in AgriTech startup and Young Smart Farmer incubation in Thailand. He in training 500+ Young Smart Farmers in northern Thailand under the New Genetic Graduate program funded by Ministry of Higher Education, Sciences and Innovation. He had 12 successful PhD completions under his direct supervision. He has published more than 100 articles in national and international journal. He is top 5 researcher in field of digital innovation at Chiang Mai University.
