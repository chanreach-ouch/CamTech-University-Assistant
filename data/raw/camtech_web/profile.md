## CamTech profile

## Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech)

vision

To be a leading university with an international reputation for academic excellence in the fields of Science and Technology.

Mission

To offer education that responds to the needs of Cambodia’s industrial development and socio-economic growth.

About us

Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech) is an upcoming world class higher education institution.

CamTech’s core values are Integrity, Respect, Excellence, Innovation, Diversity which we call IREID.

Our motto is Knowledge, Reason, Character and we proudly believe to be able to make a difference in the existing education environment in Cambodia.

We work on our academic programs aiming to bring innovation and knowledge that will contribute to the economic and social progress for the existing and the future generations.

We stand out for

- Full-time lecturers and world class researchers with PhDs and MSc, and international exposure.
- Sponsorship, internship and employment opportunities as well as exchange program with universities in ASEAN, Europe, Canada, China and Australia.
- On-campus tech startups, innovation office, and sport complex as well as network with the arts to provide venues for students to explore their talents.
- Industry linkage
- Research and Innovation team onsite

Facilities

- Smart classrooms
CamTech provides all classroom with adequate modern technologies in order to warranty the best multimedia experience both for our students and teachers.
- E-learning studio
CamTech is ready to adapt to the unpredictable changes dictated from the “new normal” that we have to be ready to face.
- Laboratory, workshop, computer and internet access…
