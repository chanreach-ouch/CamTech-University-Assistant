CamTech University and Huawei Technologies (Cambodia) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) at CamTech University

On the afternoon of June 23, 2022, CamTech University and Huawei Technologies (Cambodia) Co., Ltd signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) at CamTech University. The signing ceremony was held with the aims of (1) strengthening the cooperation between the two institutions in providing short- and long-term courses on Networking and Cloud Computing skills, (2) providing internship opportunities and scholarships for Cambodian students, and (3) co-organizing professional training, workshops, and exhibitions as well. The two institutions also aim to collaborate on research and innovation projects. CamTech University and Huawei Technologies (Cambodia) have a common vision for the development of human resources in Cambodia in the field of science and technology.
