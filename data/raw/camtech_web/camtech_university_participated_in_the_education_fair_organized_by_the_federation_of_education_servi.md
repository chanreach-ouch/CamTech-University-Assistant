CamTech University participated in the education fair organized by the Federation of Education Services in Cambodia, Real Thai Gold Solutions Co., Ltd., and Olympia Shopping Mall

From September 1st to 4th, 2022, CamTech University participated in the education fair organized by the Federation of Education Services in Cambodia, Real Thai Gold Solutions Co., Ltd., and Olympia Shopping Mall. At that event, the team of CamTech University made a presentation on the curriculum and the selection of study majors at CamTech University. At the same time, CamTech students also shared good tips for studying in the 12th grade and some experiences in choosing majors and educational institutions while continuing their studies at the university. In addition, they shared their experiences of participating in the CamTech Idea Competition (CIC) and other activities CamTech students have done, such as the Green Book Charity, business idea competitions, and sports competitions. CamTech University would like to thank the Federation of Education Services in Cambodia, Real Thai Gold Solutions Co., Ltd., and Olympia Mall for providing CamTech with the opportunity to participate in this education fair.
