On Friday, August 11th, 2023, academics, architecture and interior design students had a productive and joyful site visit to the Countryside Education Project located in Prey Veng province. This visit was organized under the New Future for Children (NFC) organization with the aim of engaging our students in community and social design solutions.

Additionally, a major counseling workshop was conducted for high school students in the community to help expand their vision when selecting a suitable major for higher education. 

Mr. Yim Akthichetha, an architecture major, gave a presentation on his study pathway and experiences at CamTech University.
