**Schedule Your Campus Visit**

Welcome to CamTech University. To keep you and the entire CamTech community safe, all visitors are required to follow the Ministry of Health’s guidelines on prevention of Covid-19 which include masks or facial coverings and social distancing.

CamTech offers two types of campus visit options:

- **Daily campus visit:** This visit introduces the prospective students who have not yet applied or been admitted to CamTech University, particularly the current high school students and high school graduates.
- **Transfer students:** This visit is for the current university students to meet individually with an admissions staff to discuss theirs transfer credits from other institutions. The visitors can also propose a campus walking tour led by an Admission staff.
