Happy Chandara School provides quality education exclusively for female students, while CamTech University supports women in STEM fields. Recently, CamTech conducted a workshop at Happy Chandara School on May 13th, 2023 and discovered that many of the female students were eager to engage with STEM subjects and stay up-to-date with recent technological and scientific advancements.

During the workshop, Ms. Ou Ketsovaddey, who majors in data science and AI engineering, and Ms. Lin Seka, an architecture major, shared their personal experiences as women in STEM at CamTech University.

We were delighted by the enthusiastic participation and engagement of all the attendees.
