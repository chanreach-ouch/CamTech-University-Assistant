CamTech students and staff to conduct a study tour at Lyly Food Industry

Dr. Alamgir Hossain led a group of 26 CamTech students and staff to conduct a study tour at Lyly Food Industry on the morning of July 15, 2022. This study tour provided the students with experience in the production process in the factory. CamTech students also gained experience in the overall operation of the production, export process, and collaboration. CamTech University would like to thank Lok Chumteav Oknha Keo Mom, Founder and General Director of Lyly Food Industry, for allowing our students to visit Lyly Food Industry in person.
