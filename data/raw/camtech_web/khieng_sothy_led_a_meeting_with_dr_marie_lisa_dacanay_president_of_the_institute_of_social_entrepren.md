On the morning of 02 June 2022, Dr. Khieng Sothy led a meeting with Dr. Marie Lisa Dacanay, President of the Institute of Social Entrepreneurship in Asia (ISEA). In this regional meeting, CamTech University and ISEA encouraged regional collaboration, including conferences and workshops on social innovation and community networking. In addition, the two institutions also urged a budget for youths to conduct their research and innovation projects.
