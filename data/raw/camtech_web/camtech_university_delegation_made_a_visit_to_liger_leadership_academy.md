CamTech University delegation made a visit to Liger Leadership Academy

A CamTech University delegation made a visit to Liger Leadership Academy on 26 August 2022 with an aim to build a good relationship between the two institutions. In the meeting, Long Mengthong, a CamTech freshman on a 100% scholarship and also a Liger alumnus, gave a presentation to the Liger management and students about learning life at CamTech as well as an internship project, scholarship opportunities, and other activities at CamTech. In addition, the CamTech team also provided a consultation session on learning activities at CamTech and made a tour around the Liger campus as well. The CamTech team would like to express our thanks to Liger Leadership Academy for their cordial welcome.
