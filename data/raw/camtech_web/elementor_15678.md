CamTech University join with KMH Foundation to organize a major selection consultation workshop and a tree-planting event at Samdech Techo Hun Sen Kampong Trabek High School

Information and knowledge on choosing a university major are very important and students look for this information to help them make the right decision. Therefore, CamTech University made a presentation on the selection of majors that are the needs of the current and future job market. CamTech students also shared their personal experiences in preparing for high school exams, choosing majors, and getting scholarships. It should be noted that CamTech University joined with KMH Foundation to organize a major selection consultation workshop and a tree-planting event at Samdech Techo Hun Sen Kampong Trabek High School on July 22, 2022.
