On 8th November 2023, CamTech management, staff and students visited Techo International Airport construction located at Kandal and Takeo province. This visit gives an opportunity to our students to learn about the construction methods, process and as well as the green architecture specially designed at this new airport. We would like to thank management of Techo International Airport for their facilitate this visit.
