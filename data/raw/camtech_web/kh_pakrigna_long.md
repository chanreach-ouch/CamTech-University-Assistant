## Pakrigna Long

Mr. Pakrigna Long has joined CamTech in March 2021 as a lecturer. He holds a master’s degree from KMITL, Thailand in the field of Computing in Engineering Systems. He has a dual bachelor’s degree; in Computer Science from NPIC, Cambodia and Informatics Engineering from STMIK IKMI Cirebon, Indonesia. Since January 2020, he has pursued his PhD in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the Universiti Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Before becoming the lecturer at the CamTech, he used to be an IT officer in Phnom Penh and a data analyst in Kuala Lumpur. His research interests are Natural Language Processing, Data mining and analytics and Renewable Energy.
