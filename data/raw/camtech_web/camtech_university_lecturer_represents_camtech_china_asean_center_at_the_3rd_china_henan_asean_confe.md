Mr. Dechkunn Chhay, Lecturer at CamTech University and representative of the **CamTech China–ASEAN Center**, proudly participated in the **3rd China (Henan)–ASEAN Conference** at Zhengzhou University, China. He presented on the topic, **“Universities as Key Actors Promoting Multilateralism between China and ASEAN,”** emphasizing the pivotal role of higher education institutions in strengthening academic collaboration, people-to-people connectivity, and sustainable partnerships across the region.

His participation reflects the commitment of the **CamTech China–ASEAN Center** to advancing educational exchange, research collaboration, and multilateral cooperation between China and ASEAN through meaningful academic engagement.
