Are you looking for the Chevening Scholarship Program 2023–2024? It is a UK government global scholarship program which is funded by the Foreign and Commonwealth Office (FCO) and partner organizations. The hybrid scholarship introduction workshop will take place at CamTech University on October 13, 2022, from 3:30-5:00 pm. Join us on Facebook livestream.
