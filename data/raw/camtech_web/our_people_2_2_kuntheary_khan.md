Ms. Khan Kuntheary earned a Master’s degree in Business Administration, specializing in Finance from National University of Management. Ms. Kuntheary has 18-year working experience in the field of accounting and finance and made great contributions to several big local and foreign-owned companies. She was an accounting manager at CIA First International School for more than 10 years, and she then moved to a new company, Ramatex Group, where she worked as an accounting manager for 2 years. Ms. Kuntheary rapidly advanced to a top position in the field of finance, holding a position as finance manager of GM Property. Ms. Kuntheary’s fast advancement in her career is attributed to her strong technical skills in accounting and finance, some of which include Quick Book, Peachtree, ERP, Conical-hate, UBS system, advanced Excel, financial analysis and reporting, Cambodian taxation. In addition, Ms. Kuntheary possesses strong management skills in coaching and training, KPI management, team management.
