CamTech University co-organized a workshop to provide consultation on university major selection and career orientation at Sovannaphumi High School Tep Phan Branch

In the morning of 20th May 2022, CamTech University co-organized a workshop to provide consultation on university major selection and career orientation at Sovannaphumi High School, Tep Phan Branch, Phnom Penh. The main purpose of this workshop was to promote the understanding and awareness of high school students of the selection of university majors that match their talents and the needs in the labor market and also to help students prepare themselves for their studies at university. In the workshop, we received 260 participants, among whom there were Sovannaphumi students from grades 11 and 12 and teachers from Bak Touk Branch, Tuok Kork Branch, and Tep Phan Branch.
