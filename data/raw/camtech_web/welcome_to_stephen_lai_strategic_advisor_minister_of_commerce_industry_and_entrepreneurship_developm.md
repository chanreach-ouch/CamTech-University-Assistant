Welcome to Stephen Lai, Strategic Advisor, Minister of Commerce, Industry and Entrepreneurship Development of Penang, Malaysia to CamTech University

Welcome to Stephen Lai, Strategic Advisor, Minister of Commerce, Industry and Entrepreneurship Development of Penang, Malaysia to CamTech University.

On Thursday, September 15, 2022, Mr. Stephen Lai visited CamTech University. During the visit, the two sides exchanged views and shared experiences, as well as agreed to establish cooperation on a number of issues, including research, and exchange of staff and students for study or internship in Penang, Malaysia. We hope that Penang, Malaysia and Cambodia will have good cooperation in the future.
