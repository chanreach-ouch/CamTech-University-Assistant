# Exchange Credit Program

CamTech currently offers the following specialization courses:

###### AI and Data Science

This program equips students with the skills to analyze complex data, design intelligent systems, and apply AI technologies across industries. Through hands-on learning in machine learning, big data, natural language processing, computer vision, and AIoT, students gain both technical depth and practical experience

###### Architecture & Interior Design

Architecture & Interior Design program prepares students to design innovative, sustainable, and culturally relevant spaces. With a balance of design studios, theory, technology, and real-world projects, students gain the skills to create architecture and interiors that address environmental, social, and technological challenges. Through hands-on studios, CAD/BIM, and collaborative projects, graduates are ready to shape the built environment with creativity and responsibility.

###### Cyber Security

Cybersecurity equips students with the technical and professional expertise to secure digital systems and defend against cyber threats. Through hands-on learning in network security, cryptography, ethical hacking, and digital forensics, students gain practical skills to protect organizations in an increasingly connected world.

###### Risk Management and Business Intelligence

The Risk Management & Business Intelligence (RMB) program prepares students to analyze data, assess risks, and drive strategic decisions in today’s dynamic business world. With a focus on business, technology, and risk, students learn to extract insights from complex data, develop innovative solutions, and communicate risk and compliance effectively.

Through specialized courses, hands-on projects, and global opportunities, graduates are equipped with the skills to tackle challenges in data visualization, fraud detection, and risk mitigation. They are ready to make an impact across industries such as finance, insurance, banking, healthcare, and government.

###### Robotics and Automation Engineering

Robotic and Automation Engineering program provides students with the skills to design, program, and control robotic and automated systems. Through training in robotics fundamentals, control systems, industrial automation, and AI integration, students gain both technical and hands-on expertise to shape the future of smart factories, autonomous systems, and advanced manufacturing.

###### Software Engineering

The Bachelor of Software Engineering at CamTech University provides students with advanced technical and professional skills to thrive in the evolving IT and software industries. You will gain a strong foundation in computing, algorithms, and system design
