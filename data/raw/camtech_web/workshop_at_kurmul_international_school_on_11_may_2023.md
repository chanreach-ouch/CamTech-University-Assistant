CamTech is thrilled to have had the opportunity to conduct a workshop at Kurmul International School on 11 May 2023, where we were pleased to find numerous students who expressed a strong interest in STEM majors.

Our workshop addressed this important topic, and resulted in an engaging discussion with the students, who raised several thought-provoking questions about Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, and other related topics.

The students at Kurmul International School are exceptionally talented, active, and well-known for their interactive engagement during the workshop, which was truly inspiring. Additionally, our own CamTech student speakers, Leak Chanmolika and Sarun Rinviphou, delivered captivating presentations that touched upon the importance of choosing STEM-related majors and shared helpful tips and insights into preparing for the Grade 12 national exam and navigating university life. They also showcased their own learning and internship programs.
