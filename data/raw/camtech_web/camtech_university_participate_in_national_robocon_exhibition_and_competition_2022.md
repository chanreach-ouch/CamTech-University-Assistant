CamTech University participate in National Robocon Exhibition and Competition 2022

On June 25 and 26, 2022, a team of students from CamTech University participated in National Robocon Exhibition and Competition 2022. This is CamTech University’s first national robocon competition.

CamTech 01 is one team among the other teams from five competing universities and institutes. CamTech 01 team put both physical and mental efforts, patience, and persistence. The competition brought solidarity, teamwork, friendships, and great experiences. This is the pride of CamTech that CamTech first-year students participated in this competition.
