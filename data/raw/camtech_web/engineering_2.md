**The Professional Diploma Program (PDP)** at Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech) is designed for a career-focused, accomplished associate degree or the equivalent. Student enrolled in the Professional Diploma will elevate their focus expertise  to a professional level and learn critical skills in entrepreneurship, freelancing work, and other business-related skills. PDP provides a passway to a full bachelor’s program after students complete the program and fulfill the requirements for their chosen major. Students enrolled in this program will identify a focus area that complements their major area studies and supports the development of their professional goals. This program gives students the opportunity to deepen and expand their readiness to enter and be leaders within ever-changing specialties by broadening their skill sets and access to a variety of techniques ready for work. Their coursework will prepare them to engage in repertoire planning that demands more diversity in programming, addressing current needs and gaps in the professional world. The program includes a career-focused seminar, core coursework, and student-selected job placement learning, in addition to private lessons, large ensembles, and elective courses. The 2-year Professional Diploma program contains 80 credits combining 70 credits of specialized coursework and 12 credits of internship and 4 credits for the final diploma project report.
