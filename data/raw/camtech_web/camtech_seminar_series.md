“AI Forum Research Seminar Series is another CamTech’s initiative to promote research culture and practice at higher education institutions in Cambodia. The Seminar Series is also a high-level academic seminar designed to provide opportunities for local and international scholars, researchers, academics, faculty members, scientists, and students to present their research studies and receive valuable feedback from experts in the field of AI, Cyber Security, and Digitalization to improve their work. The Series also undertakes to offer a welcoming, nonpartisan, and stimulating atmosphere, allowing speakers, discussants, and the general audience to interact with one another and engage in the same learning process.”
