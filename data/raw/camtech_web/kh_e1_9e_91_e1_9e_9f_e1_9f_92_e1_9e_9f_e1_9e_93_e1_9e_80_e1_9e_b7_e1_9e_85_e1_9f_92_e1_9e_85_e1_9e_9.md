The Vice President of CamTech University paid a study visit to the National Polytechnic Institute of Cambodia (NPIC)

Communication and cooperation to build partnership with other education institutions are important to CamTech University. In this essence, CamTech students led by Professor Alamgir Hossain, Vice President of CamTech University, paid a study visit to the National Polytechnic Institute of Cambodia (NPIC) on the morning of August 15, 2022, to learn about the training system of the Institute for Social Development, to build a partnership between CamTech and NPIC, and to discuss possible collaboration on future projects. The students also visited the NPIC campus. CamTech University would like to thank the leadership of the National Polytechnic Institute of Cambodia for the warm welcome toward CamTech representatives and students.
