CamTech University organize a workshop at Sovannaphumi School in Sihanouk Ville Province

In order to encourage students to be prepared before entering the university and to raise awareness related to university majors, especially STEM majors, CamTech University organized a workshop at Sovannaphumi School in Sihanouk Ville Province to provide university major consultation and career orientation for the future.

The workshop was held on the afternoon of June 30, 2022, with the participation of 44 teachers and students.

In this workshop, CamTech students also shared their learning experiences and presented various projects such as AR / VR Games to the students at Sovannaphumi School.
