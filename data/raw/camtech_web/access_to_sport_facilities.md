At CamTech, we believe that education is not only about gaining knowledge and professional skills, but also about nurturing healthy, balanced, and resilient individuals. President Dr. Khieng Sothy strongly values the role of sport in building both a strong body and a strong mind, encouraging students to pursue excellence both inside and outside the classroom.

Alumni can enjoy access to selected sports and recreational facilities, including the gym, yoga room, volleyball and basketball courts, table tennis, and snooker facilities.
