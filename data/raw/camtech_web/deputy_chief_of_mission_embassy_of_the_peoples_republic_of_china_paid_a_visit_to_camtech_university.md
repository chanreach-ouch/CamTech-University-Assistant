On the afternoon of 23 June 2022, Mr. Chang Jian, Deputy Chief of Mission, Embassy of the People’s Republic of China, paid a visit to CamTech university. In this meeting, the two parties discussed research collaboration and exchange of human resource between CamTech university and universities from China. Besides, Mr. Chang Jian also visited CamTech campus in order to know more about classroom preparation, atmosphere and infrastructure.
