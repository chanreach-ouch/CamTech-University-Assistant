Together with the Techo Startup Center, CamTech hosted a workshop entitled “Start a Startup” on April 22, 2022, which was open to both CamTech students and the public. The objective of the workshop was to share fundamental knowledge on how to begin a new business. The presentation also covered a case study of a successful project. CamTech students were also encouraged to participate in the Turing Hackathon competition which is also organized by the Techo Startup Center, Ministry of Economy and Finance.
