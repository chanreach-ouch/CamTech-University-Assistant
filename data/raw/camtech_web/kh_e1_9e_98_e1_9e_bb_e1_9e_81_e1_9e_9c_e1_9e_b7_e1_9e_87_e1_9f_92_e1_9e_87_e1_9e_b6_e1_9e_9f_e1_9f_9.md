The fast-track preparatory programs at CamTech University are non-degree programs that are designed to help learners prepare for academic studies or employment. We offer three programs, including the General English Program (GEP), the English for Academic Purposes (EAP), and the Pre-University Program (PUP).

The GEP is a comprehensive language learning program that focuses on improving the four key language skills.

The EAP is designed to help non-native speakers of English improve their academic language skills.

PUP is a preparatory program that helps high school graduates to meet the academic requirements for direct entry into their desired university programs.

Each program has different levels and hours, and learners can choose the program that suits their needs.
