CamTech University has applied the American credit system. The number of study hours per course a student is expected to be successfully completed within a term. This helps students to move between universities with global recognition of academic qualification. For coursework, it is normally accounted with 15 hours per credit. But for a fieldwork-based course or internship, it requires 45 hours per credit.
