CamTech participated in a career fair at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia and the college fair at Rodwell Learning Center

Students can seek advice on choosing a career in higher education and future career orientation with CamTech University.

On August 20 and 21, 2022, CamTech participated in a career fair at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia and the college fair at Rodwell Learning Center to provide professional advice to young people who wish to pursue their higher education, both bachelor’s and master’s degrees. CamTech provides up to 75 full STEM scholarships for students. CamTech also provides an opportunity for young students who aim to do internships at CamTech University to develop their skills and practical work experience.

Please note that those who wish to know the details of each major can send a message to the Facebook page and the Telegram channel of CamTech University.
