    # Risk Management & Business Intelligence

### Course description

The Risk Management & Business Intelligence (RMB) program prepares students to analyze data, assess risks, and drive strategic decisions in today’s dynamic business world. With a focus on business, technology, and risk, students learn to extract insights from complex data, develop innovative solutions, and communicate risk and compliance effectively. Through specialized courses, hands-on projects, and global opportunities, graduates are equipped with the skills to tackle challenges in data visualization, fraud detection, and risk mitigation. They are ready to make an impact across industries such as finance, insurance, banking, healthcare, and government.
