CamTech University organized a workshop on "University Major Selection Consultation and Career Orientation for the Future" at Sovanna Phumi School Branch 2 in Siem Reap

On June 27th, 2022, CamTech University organized a workshop on “University Major Selection Consultation and Career Orientation for the Future” at Sovanna Phumi School, Siem Reap, Branch 2, with the participation of 40 teachers and students. The purpose of this workshop was to raise awareness of university majors, especially STEM majors that are relevant to current and future market needs, and to help prepare students for university. In addition, CamTech students shared their learning experiences with the presentation of various projects as well as AR / VR Games to Sovannaphumi students.
