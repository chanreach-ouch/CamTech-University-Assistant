- SmartStart Unipreneur Learning Platform

CamTech University officially became a university partner of SmartStart Unipreneur in 2022. This program is implemented by Impact Hub and supported by Smart Axiata. It empowers youth and startups in Cambodia to address social and environmental problems through entrepreneurship and leadership.

**Objective of SmartStart Unipreneur**

- **E-Learning Platform:** Provide an E-Learning Platform for students to encourage self-taught learning about technopreneurship. The program combines videos, exercises, and visual materials for a more engaging, blended learning experience for university students. It will be implemented at seventeen universities as part of an accredited university curriculum.
- **National Hackathon:** Host a National Hackathon to provide a practical application of classroom knowledge, enabling students to address real-world challenges in Cambodia and build innovative solutions.
- **Educator Training:** Provide training to educators to effectively implement the E-Learning Platform.
