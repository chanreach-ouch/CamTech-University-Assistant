CamTech University is very please to welcome Mr. Ye Xingyi, the president of ASEAN College at Guangxi Minzu University

On August 16, 2023, CamTech University is very please to welcome Mr. Ye Xingyi, the president of ASEAN College at Guangxi Minzu University. This visit provided an opportunity for both universities to engage in meaningful discussions regarding potential collaborations in various areas, such as student and staff exchanges, as well as research collaborations with a specific focus on ASEAN and Cambodia. We are confident that this collaboration will provide both institutions with the chance to enhance their teaching and learning, as well as research capabilities, in the region, thereby further strengthening their development.
