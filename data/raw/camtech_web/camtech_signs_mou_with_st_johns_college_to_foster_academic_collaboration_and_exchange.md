November 21, 2024 – CamTech is excited to announce that its delegates recently held a productive meeting with St. John’s College, located in Annapolis, Maryland, which is over 300 years old and recognized as the third oldest college in the United States.

The college is known for its distinctive great books curriculum and its commitment to liberal arts education, which emphasizes critical thinking, rigorous dialogue, and a deep exploration of foundational texts in Western civilization.

During this meeting, the two institutions signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to establish a collaborative partnership aimed at enhancing academic cooperation. This partnership will include initiatives such as:

– Faculty and Student Exchange Programs

– Joint Academic Initiatives

– 3+1 Joint Degree Programs for undergraduate and graduate studies
