Pass or fail the grade-12 national exam is a problem? Don’t know what major to study?

Don’t worry. CamTech University has developed Professional Diploma programs in Professional Software Engineering specializing in:

1. Artificial Intelligence Technology

2. AR/ VR App

3. Game App

4. Mobile App Development

5. Software Engineering in Web Development

Regardless of whether you pass or fail the grade-12 national exam, you can apply to study in this program, which spend 2 years. Students are exposed not only to learning in classes but also to practical projects and job placement at companies.
