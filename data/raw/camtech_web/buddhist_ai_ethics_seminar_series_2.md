On August 1, 2024, CamTech University hosted the second installment of the Buddhist AI Ethics Seminar Series, featuring Venerable Dr. San Pisith, a renowned Cambodian Buddhist Monk Scholar. The seminar, titled “Buddhist Governance: Historical and Contemporary Perspectives from Cambodia and Beyond,” brought together a distinguished panel of experts to examine the intricate relationships between Buddhism, governance, and modern public theory.
