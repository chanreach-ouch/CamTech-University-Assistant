The winner of the 100% Stem Talent Scholarship for the bachelor's degree in the academic year 2022-2023 at CamTech University

Sea Sok Chamroeun is the winner of the 100% Stem Talent Scholarship for the bachelor’s degree in the academic year 2022-2023 at CamTech University. It should be noted that Sea Sok Chamroeun is currently studying at Preah Monivong High School, Battambang Province, and took entrance exams to CamTech in June 2022. Chamroeun will continue his studies at CanTech University in the term which will start on January 30, 2023, when he passes the high school exams.
