On behalf of CamTech, Dr. CHEA Vatana, Director in charge of research and innovative has attended a robotic exhibition held between 17-19 December 2021 and brought a group of students of Preah Monivong high school some gifts from the university to motivate them on their endeavor. As always, CamTech is more than willing to support talented and innovative students by providing them scholarships to continue their bachelor degree in STEM majors, assistantship, and other types of financial and emotional aids to make university life a period for passion exploration and a meaningful experience.
