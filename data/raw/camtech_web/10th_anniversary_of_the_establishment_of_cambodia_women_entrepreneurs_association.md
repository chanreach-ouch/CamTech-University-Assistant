Congratulation on the 10th anniversary of the establishment of Cambodia Women Entrepreneurs Association, which took place on March 31, 2022. CamTech University wishes the CWEA and all women entrepreneurs to be healthy and successful in their business.

Women play important role in the development of the nation. Therefore, CamTech supports women in STEM education by providing 50 STEM scholarships to women annually .
