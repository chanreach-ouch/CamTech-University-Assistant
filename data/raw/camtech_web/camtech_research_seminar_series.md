On 19<sup>th</sup> August 2021 CamTech University hosted its first Research Seminar, on the topic “Sovereignty and Diplomacy in Cyberspace: A Philosophical Inquiry” presented by Mr. Chhem Siriwat. The seminar was moderated by Dr. Chea Vatana, Director of the Center for Professional Education and Training, CamTech University. The seminar was also honored to have two esteemed discussants, H.E. Mr. Tean Samnang and Mr. Ou Phannarith. The discussants as well as participants provided valuable comments and insights on the topic.

CamTech Research Seminar Series is part of CamTech University’s commitment to promote research culture in Cambodian society, especially higher education institutions.
