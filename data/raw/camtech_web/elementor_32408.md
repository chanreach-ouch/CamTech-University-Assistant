A Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed between CamTech University and Shenzhen Polytechnic University in Guangdong Province, China on September 13, 2023. The MoU aims to promote cooperation between the two universities in various areas, including research projects, student exchange programs, entrepreneurship and incubation initiatives, knowledge sharing, and joint educational activities. Additionally, the MoU involves organizing informative seminars, workshops, and guest lectures either online or in-person, which will benefit both universities
