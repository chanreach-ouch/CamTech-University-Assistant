On September 11, 2023, CamTech University had signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Jiangsu College of Engineering and Technology (JCET) in Jiangsu Province, China. This exciting event marks a significant milestone in the partnership between the two institutions. The primary objective of this MoU is to foster cooperation and assistance between CamTech University and Jiangsu College of Engineering and Technology in a range of fields, including research initiatives, student exchange programs, knowledge transfer, and joint educational events. Additionally, the MoU entails organizing informative online or in-person seminars, workshops, and guest lectures that will be advantageous for both institutions.
