## CamTech University Press

CamTech University Press is an academic publishing platform that facilitates the publication of articles in peer-reviewed journals, with a primary focus on Science, Technology, and Sustainability (STS). It is dedicated to exploring the interplay of science and technology within the context of sustainability, adopting an interdisciplinary approach that incorporates perspectives from history, economics, political science, international relations, science policy, innovation, technology management, governance, and environmental studies. The press aims to advance scholarly research and discourse in these areas, promoting rigorous, peer-reviewed contributions that address the challenges and opportunities of sustainable development through science and technology.
