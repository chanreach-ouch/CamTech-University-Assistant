CamTech president paid a courtesy visit to H.E. Dr. Hang Chuon Naron Minister of Education Youth, and Sport

On Monday morning, 02 May 2022, a delegation from CamTech University, led by Dr. Khieng Sothy, CamTech president, paid a courtesy visit to H.E. Dr. Hang Chuon Naron, Minister of Education, Youth, and Sport. In the meeting, both sides emphasized the significance of science and technology education as a catalyst to propel development in Cambodia. H.E. Dr. Hang Chuon Naron addressed university should provide strong foundations for students, create students’ curiosity, and promote lifelong learning and entrepreneurship. Dr. Khieng Sothy, CamTech president also requested future collaboration between the Ministry of Education and CamTech on digital transformation for university and creating a modern and digitalized learning ecosystem through research grants.
