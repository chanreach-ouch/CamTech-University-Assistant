Mr. Vladimir Lazarov has lived in Europe, Africa, and Australia but calls Cambodia his home country. He has more than 11 years of experience teaching English to local university and high school students. Mr. Vladimir Lazarov strongly believes that understanding the Khmer culture and values is an important aspect of providing effective education. He is driven by passion and desire to make a difference. Teaching is a very rewarding career that gives a sense of fulfillment where the learning process extends beyond the limits of a textbook or geographical borders. Knowledge is boundless, and by teaching others, we learn more and eventually become better human beings.
