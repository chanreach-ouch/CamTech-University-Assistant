CamTech held its fifth “CamTech Going Green” event on September 30th, 2023, with the aim of promoting environmental conservation and emphasizing the importance of tree planting. Alongside the CamTech community, 80 students from Sovannaphumi school participated in the event, contributing to the planting of 400 trees and tidying up the CamTech campus’s surroundings.
