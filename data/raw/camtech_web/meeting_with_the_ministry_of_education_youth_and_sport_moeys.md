On March 14, 2025, the Future Skills Mission to Cambodia, led by H.E. San Vathana, Undersecretary of State at the Ministry of Education, Youth, and Sports, brought together leaders from public and private universities to meet with the Australian Embassy in Phnom Penh and Austrade representatives.

The discussion aims to foster institutional partnerships, support Cambodia’s long-term education strategy, and explore innovative learning solutions for the future.

CamTech University is honored to be part of this significant discussion, represented by Prof. Dr. Sothy Khieng, Founding President, and Prof. Dr. Leng Phirom, Vice President for Academic Affairs.
