# Chip Mong Group and CamTech University’s Collaboration on Scholarships, Internships, and Employment Opportunities

January 29, 2022 2022-01-29 11:52
On Friday, January 28, the Chip Mong Group team, led by Dr. Im Sothearith, Director of the Human Resource Department, came to visit CamTech University’s campus to talk about potential areas of collaboration. CamTech University and the Chip Mong Group had a very fruitful and dynamic discussion as the two parties shared a similar vision and mission.

CamTech University aims to be a leading university in the fields of science and technology to guarantee Cambodia’s economic development and future prosperity and to offer education that responds to the needs of industrial development and socio-economic growth.

Chip Mong Group is one of the well-established corporations in Cambodia that has contributed significantly to the economic growth of the country. Chip Mong Group’s core values include “Collaboration with Commitment”, “Making a Difference”, “Growing and Giving Back” and “Customer Focus” (CMGC).

Chip Mong Group’s core values, particularly “Growing and Giving Back”, are well-aligned with CamTech’s objective of producing young talents in order to support the economic growth and welfare of Cambodia. Thus, CamTech University is pleased to announce a partnership with the Chip Mong Group to provide STEM Genius Scholarships, Internships, and Employment Opportunities for young potential Cambodian talents.
