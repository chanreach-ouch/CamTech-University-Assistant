CamTech University stands on roughly five hectares of land, on which there is the main campus, a sports complex, a residence area, a canteen, and a demining robot office.

The main campus is equipped with modern offices, pantry rooms, a book shop, a sports therapy room, smart classrooms Type A, smart classrooms Type B, a computer laboratory, an e-learning room, a Data center room, lecture halls, a robot maker space, start-up rooms, and a seminar hall.

The university sports complex hosts an Olympic standard football pitch, volleyball courts, tennis courts, table tennis tables, basketball courts, a baseball pitch, a swimming pool, a modern gym, a yoga room, a field house, and a medical center.
