CamTech University students have a study visit to the headquarters of Chip Mong Bank

Collaboration between universities and industry partners is a bridge to provide knowledge to students through a study visit at that industry partner and through the transfer of the latest technology to each other. Recognizing the importance of engaging with industry partners, CamTech University had 40 students go on a study visit to the headquarters of Chip Mong Bank, a university’s industrial partner, on August 3, 2022. The visit provided students with an in-depth understanding of the experience of starting a business, company structuring, and the company’s use latest technology, as well as internship opportunities for the students. CamTech University would like to thank the management of Chipmong Group for allowing the students to participate in this useful visit. The university will continue to visit other industry partners in the future.
