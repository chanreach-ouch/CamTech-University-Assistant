The Ambassador of India paid a courtesy visit to CamTech university

On the evening of June 13, 2022, the ambassador of India, Dr. Devyani Uttam Khobragade, paid a courtesy visit to CamTech university. During this visit, Dr. Devyani Uttam Khobragade discussed the opportunities for collaboration and mutual support between CamTech and the Embassy of India in Cambodia such as research, staff, and student exchange programs, including a visit to the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay and other technology institutes in India. At the end of this visit, she also thanked CamTech for a warm welcome and hoped to see both institutions have good relations.
