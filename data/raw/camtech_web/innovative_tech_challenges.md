The Innovative Tech Challenge is designed to encourage innovative and practical tech solutions that address Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and promote an entrepreneurial mindset among undergraduate students at higher learning institutions in Cambodia, including both universities and Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) schools.

This program has been organized by CamTech University since 2023. The first season of the Innovative Tech Challenge was supported by Khmer Enterprise and UNESCAP, while the second season received support from Khmer Enterprise and the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology & Innovation (MISTI).

Innovative Tech Challenge Season 2, under the theme of “Building the Innovative Future Today,” was launched in June 2024. For more details about Season 2, click link below https://sites.google.com/camtech.edu.kh/innovative-tech-challenge/home
