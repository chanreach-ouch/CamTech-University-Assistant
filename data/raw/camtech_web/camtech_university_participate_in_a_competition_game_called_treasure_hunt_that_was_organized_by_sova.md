CamTech University participate in a competition game called "Treasure Hunt" that was organized by Sovann Phumi School

On Thursday June 09 2022 CamTech University participated in a competition game called “Treasure Hunt” that was organized by Sovann Phumi School.

The purpose of this Treasure Hunt was to promote the attention and intelligence of grade-3 to grade-6 students to enhance their creativity in solving problems. In the event, CamTech students also added a game called “Spaghetti and Marshmallows Construction Game” which aimed to test students’ ability in STEM.
