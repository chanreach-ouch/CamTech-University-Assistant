Tux Global Institute (TGI) visit CamTech University

On the evening of June 7, 2022, delegates from Tux Global Institute (TGI) visited CamTech University. The purpose of this meeting is to strengthen the cooperation between the two institutions in the field of science and technology of students in Cambodia. Mr. Yusunoki Shoji, chairman of TUX Global Institute (TGI), expressed his pleasure that his institute has built partnership with CamTech University to further strengthen the science and technology skills of Cambodian students and hope in the future Cambodia, with the contribution of TGI and CamTech, will be full of young talents.

CamTech always promotes STEM to equip students with the right knowledge and skills that are relevant to the job market.
