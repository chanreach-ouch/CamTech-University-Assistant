CamTech University delegates participated in the 60th Anniversary celebration of the Center for International Studies at Ohio University from November 16-19, 2024. During this event, they served as panelists on November 19, discussing US-Cambodia Higher Education Collaboration and the importance of strategic partnerships in higher education

On the same day, CamTech University signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Ohio University, outlining key areas of collaboration, including:

– Joint Research Activities

– Faculty and Student Exchange Programs

– 3+1 Student Programs (undergraduate programs)

– Scholarship Opportunities for Graduate Programs

This agreement aims to enhance educational opportunities and promote reciprocal exchanges between the two
