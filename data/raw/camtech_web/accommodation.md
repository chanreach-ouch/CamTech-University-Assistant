## Accommodation Information

As a guide, the recommended monthly living cost for a single student in Phnom Penh is around USD $300–$400. Below is a breakdown of estimated monthly costs for common student expenses.

| What you’ll need | How much it costs | 
|---|---|
| Accommodation | ~$150 – $200 | 
| Food | ~$150 – $200 | 
| Utilities: water, electricity and gas | ~$10 – $20 | 
| Internet | ~ $0 ( included ) | 
| Transportation | ~ $40 – $100 | 

**Student Housing service available**)

All prices in this guide are shown in US dollars (USD) unless otherwise stated. Prices are approximate and subject to change.

## Living in PhnomPenh

Wherever your studies take you, with CamTech University you’ll find a supportive and dynamic home in Phnom Penh. Located in the growing Chroy Chongvar Satellite City, our campus offers safe, modern, and affordable options for international students. You can enjoy a stress-free living experience close to the city’s vibrant culture, cafés, and services.

Whether you want to stay year-round or only during your study sessions, CamTech offers a variety of options: from nearby modern condominiums (through our Canadia Group partnership) to apartments with shared facilities. You’ll be at the heart of the action – just minutes away from Phnom Penh’s riverside, cultural landmarks, shops, and student hangouts.
