The Manner International School (grade 4-10th) paid a visit to CamTech University

As a STEM-focused university, CamTech University inspires a lot of young Cambodian talents. On Thursday morning, 8th September 2022, students from the Manner International School (grade 4-10th) paid a visit to CamTech University. The students had a lot of fun engaging in virtual games and demonstrations of basic coding program introduced by CamTech students.
