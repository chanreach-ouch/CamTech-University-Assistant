CamTech is very grateful to have Ms. Kim Tol Tan, Founder of Grow4Growth Consultant Co. Ltd, as our guest speaker for the Innovative Tech Challenge program. Participants learned a lot from Ms. Kim, and they are excited to apply what they have learned to their goals and startups. They believe that with a growth mindset they can overcome any obstacle and achieve anything. Thank you, Ms. Kim, for your valuable and inspiring session.
