To respond to educational disruption caused by the COVID-19 Pandemic, CamTech has prepared the knowledge sharing session to provide insight on how to study online effectively and keep oneself mentally healthy. The sharing session is part of our policies to assist high school as well as university students to stay in good health physically and mentally during the pandemic. We believe that the sharing session will contribute to help students focus and perform well on their study.


The first sharing session was delivered on August 19, 2021 for the Tribal Education Group by Dr. CHEA Vatana, Director of the Center for Professional Education and Training. The Cambodia University of Technology and Science would like to sincerely thank the Tribal Education Group for their coordination.
