CamTech University was delighted to host Singapore (Cambodia) International Academy (SCIA) top management team and delegates on May 17th, 2023.

The purpose of the visit was to explore potential collaborations, student projects, workshops, seminars, and webinars that could benefit both students and society in Cambodia. The discussions were productive and focused on contributing to STEM education.

We express our gratitude to SCIA for taking the time to visit CamTech University.
