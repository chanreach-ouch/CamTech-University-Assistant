Open House held by CamTech university on 28th October 2022.

The purposes of this program are to: 1) Welcome students to visit CamTech campus and learn about its curriculum and learning facilities; 2) Provide counseling on major selection in higher education; and 3) Promote self-preparation for higher education.

This program will provide an important experience and insightful knowledge shared by CamTech faculty members and students as well as our industrial partners to the participants, especially the 12th graders. The participants have also engaged in fun activities.
