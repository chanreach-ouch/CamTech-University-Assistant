## China-ASEAN Studies Centre (CASC)

China-ASEAN Studies Centre (CASC) serves as a critical platform to advance mutual understanding, co-develop policy solutions, and strengthen people-to-people ties for a peaceful, stable, and prosperous region.

The primary objectives of the China-ASEAN Studies Centre are:

• Promote Dialogue and Consultation: Foster open communication and constructive dialogue among policymakers, scholars, and civil society from China and ASEAN member states to address shared challenges and opportunities.

• Enhance Mutual Trust and Understanding: Build bridges of understanding and trust between diverse stakeholders to promote mutual respect, cultural exchange, and closer cooperation.

• Facilitate Policy Solutions: Co-create and co-develop innovative, evidence-based policy solutions to address pressing regional issues such as peace, security, development, and governance. 

• Strengthen People-to-People Ties: Cultivate a deeper sense of community through cultural exchanges, academic collaborations, and shared learning opportunities.
