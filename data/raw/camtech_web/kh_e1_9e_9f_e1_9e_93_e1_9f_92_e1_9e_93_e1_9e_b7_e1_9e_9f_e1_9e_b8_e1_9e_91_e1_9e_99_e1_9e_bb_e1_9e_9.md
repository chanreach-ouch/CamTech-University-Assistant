ASEAN Youth Conference-AYC is being organized at CamTech University, Phnom Penh, from 07th to 09th October 2022. Approximately 100 ASEAN youths are participating in this event. The topic of this year’s youth conference is “Post-Covid 19 Development Cooperation”. The conference focuses on youths’ involvement in the implementation of the principles for peace, stability, solidarity, unity, prosperity, and sustainability in the ASEAN region under Cambodia’s ASEAN chairmanship to address the theme “ASEAN working together: Addressing Challenges Together”.
