The Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech) and the Comparative Education Society of Cambodia (CESCam) are co-hosting a research seminar on Envisioning Cambodia Education 2030: Diffusion of Technology in Education on November 22, 2021 at 10am-12pm.

The purpose of the seminar is to provide a platform for researchers, academics, scholars, government officials, teachers, and practitioners to discuss the challenges in and possible solutions to education in Cambodia. The seminar also aims to raise public awareness on the significance, benefits and challenges of the use of technology in teaching and learning to improve student’s outcome and to promote public-private partnership. 

The seminar will begin with a triggering presentation and followed by a panel discussion. To participate, please scan the QR code or use the meeting ID and password presented in the poster. The seminar will also be lived on Facebook.
