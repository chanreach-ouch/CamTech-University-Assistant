The Office of Student Affairs aims to offer accessible services during your time at CamTech University. Our range of student services includes academic and administrative support, student records, and financial assistance.

Administrative Services

- Student Status Letter
- Student Cards
- Lost and Found
- Scholarships

Types of Scholarships

- Women in STEM Scholarship
Supporting women in STEM fields by bridging financial gaps, creating supportive communities, and nurturing diverse talent. This empowers women to excel in STEM and contribute their brilliance to the future.
- Regional Equity Scholarship
Offering financial aid, academic support, and community engagement for students from remote, rural, and underserved areas, including indigenous and tribal communities.
- STEM Genius Scholarship
Recognizing and nurturing the brightest minds in fields like mathematics, physics, robotics, and coding. We empower these students to reach greater heights through our academic and innovation programs.
- President’s Athletic Scholarship
Bridging the gap between athletic achievement and academic excellence. This scholarship provides financial support and mentorship, allowing students to excel in both athletics and STEM fields.

Student Loan

Providing financial assistance to help students cover the costs associated with higher education at CamTech University, enabling them to access and complete their educational programs.
