Annual Student Conference on Social Innovation and Entrepreneurship

Call for abstracts. Both oral and poster presentations from undergraduate students are welcome. CamTech is hosting the Annual Student Conference on Social Innovation and Entrepreneurship to be held on October 20th, 2022. Interested presenters can submit their abstracts and/or full papers for both oral and poster presentations. Accepted papers will be published in the CamTech Annual Proceeding, and presenters are also eligible for the training workshop on “Moving from Idea to Activities and Impact”. There are also awards for best papers and best posters.
