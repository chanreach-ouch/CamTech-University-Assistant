In an era defined by artificial intelligence, CamTech University emerges as a bold force for transformation — Cambodia’s first AI-driven university and a trailblazer in the region. At CamTech, AI is our foundation. We are the first in Cambodia to offer a complete academic pathway in **AI — from bachelor’s** to PhD — taught by dedicated, full-time faculty who combine deep technical expertise with a passion for teaching and discovery.

But we go further. CamTech is the first university in Asia to offer a **PhD in Science, Technology, and Society (STS)** — a groundbreaking program that prepares students to lead at the intersection of innovation, ethics, and social change. CamTech hosts three distinct but complementary initiatives that support education, **research**, and values-based innovation in artificial intelligence. The **AI Forum (AIF)** is a pioneering platform founded in 2018 to promote critical dialogue on AI’s societal impact. **The Center for Buddhist AI Ethics** brings ancient wisdom into conversation with cutting-edge technology. The AI Lab for Education is a hands-on, innovation-driven laboratory focused on applying AI to enhance learning and teaching.
