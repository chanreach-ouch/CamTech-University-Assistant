The Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech) has received official recognition through Sub-Decree No. 44, អនក.បក, dated March 22, 2021, from the Royal Government of Cambodia. Our university offers a range of undergraduate programs, including Associate and Bachelor’s degrees, as well as graduate programs focused on Science, Technology, and Sustainability (STS) that cater to the needs of Cambodia’s industrial development and socio-economic growth.

The Foundation Year Curriculum has been accredited by the Accreditation Committee of Cambodia (ACC) under accreditation number 57/22.គ.ទ.ក/សជណ, dated October 31, 2022, enabling the delivery of our undergraduate programs.

Furthermore, the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS) officially licensed our graduate programs on October 6, 2023. This milestone underscores our commitment to delivering high-quality education to our students and reinforces our dedication to meeting the evolving demands of the modern workforce.

At CamTech, we adhere to the standards and guidelines set forth by MoEYS and the Accreditation Committee of Cambodia (ACC). Our academic programs align with the National Qualification Framework, and we implement an academic credit system to provide a comprehensive and enriching educational experience for our students.
