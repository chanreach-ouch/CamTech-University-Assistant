Ms. Nguon Pichvoleak is an Academic Affairs officer at CamTech University. She held a bachelor’s degree in International Relations from Institute For International Studies and Public Policy, Royal University of Phnom Penh. After graduation, she joined Teach For Cambodia as a teaching fellow at Chea Sim Chrong Chongva High school and pursued a Master of Education in Curriculum and Instruction. She has years of experiences in teaching kids and teenagers on digital literacy and drag-drop coding. Before joining CamTech University, she was a senior curriculum and training officer at Tech For Kids Academy where she developed coding curriculum for two courses and trained new teachers on pedagogy including the principle of effective planning, the principle of instructions, classroom management, and more.
