Faculty members of CamTech have brought honor to our university through their interventions in high-level international forums. At the Aristotle-Confucius Symposium on Ethics at Tsinghua University, Beijing, Prof Chhem Kieth Rethy and Prof Khieng Sothy presented their respective talks on how Buddhist wisdoms can guide contemporary challenges such as AI and sustainability.

This engagement is a testament to their groundbreaking research and its global impact. During their time in China, they participated in the Nishan Forum on World Civilizations. This is a remarkable achievement that highlights our university’s commitment to fostering intellectual leadership and global engagement, as a member of the United Nations Sustainable Development Solution Network (SDSN).
