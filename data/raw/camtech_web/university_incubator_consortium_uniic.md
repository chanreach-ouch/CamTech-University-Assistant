- University Incubator Consortium (UNIIC)

CamTech University is thrilled to announce its official membership in the University Incubator Consortium (UNIIC) as of 2023. In May 2024, we proudly signed the UNIIC Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) alongside universities from ASEAN countries, India, and South Africa.

The University Incubator Consortium (UNIIC) collaborates with university partners across the ASEAN, Indian, and African regions. Its objective is to enhance capabilities in assisting startups, facilitate the exchange of knowledge and skills, and foster resource-sharing.

UNIIC offers a diverse range of opportunities for participants to advance their business ideas, including:

- **Mutual Visits:** Student and staff exchange programs
- **Access to Support and Facilities:** Use of state-of-the-art incubation centers
- **Joint Events:** Organizing ideation bootcamps, competitions, and accelerator programs
- **Mentorship and Training:** Providing mentor sharing and training sessions for startups and spin-off companies
- **Advisory Services:** Guidance on regulatory requirements and market understanding
- **Investment Guidance:** Assistance with venture capitalists or angel investors
- **Collaborative Research:** Joint research projects in entrepreneurship
- **Curriculum Sharing:** Exchange of curriculum and teaching materials related to entrepreneurship programs
- **Program Development:** Creating new programs related to entrepreneurship
