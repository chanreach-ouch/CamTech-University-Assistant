CamTech University organized a workshop on "University Major Selection Consultation and Career Orientation for the Future" at Smart Kids International School Siem Reap branch

To strengthen cooperation in research, capacity building, student exchange, internship, and partnership between university and other academic institutions, CamTech, Asian Vision Institute (AVI), and Tribal Education Group (TEG) signed a Memorandum of Understanding on April 6, 2022. CamTech has been working to contribute to building an innovation and entrepreneurial ecosystem to support science, technology, and socio-economic development of Cambodia
