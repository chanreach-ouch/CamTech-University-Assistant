## Why CamTech is here to make history

## Neak Oknha Dr. Pung Kheav Se

###### Chairman of the Board of Trustees


						
- Cambodia’s economy is moving quickly and now more than ever local education system needs to develop and support the growth of local talents in fields of modern technologies, science. engineering, social sciences, arts and 
Understanding these urgent needs, CamTech is here to lead the enhancement of the most demanded technical disciplines, providing students, adults and busines professional with the best of academics, certified professional courses and workshops.
- CamTech knows that, according to a recent study, students learning science and technology related fields are only account for 20% of all university students in Cambodia while those studying fields like languages, banking and finance, accounting, hospitality are around the 73%. 
Realistically the market demand of data analysts, data scientists, media and marketing specialists, robot automation technicians, AI solutions, machine learning tools is increasing day by day, while the most of other sectors are living one of the biggest world crisis from which we will not recover so fast.
- CamTech is aware, looking at the countries in our economic region, that Cambodia has to hurry up to reach the same level of civil and economic development.
In particular CamTech wants to give equal opportunities to talented Cambodians inspiring, motivating, supporting and training them to become active players to reach the prosperous future that the country deserve.

As Cambodia’s economy continues to grow rapidly, our education system must evolve to support the development of local talent in critical fields such as modern technology, science, engineering, social sciences, and the arts. Recognizing this urgent need, CamTech University is committed to leading the way in building the most in-demand technical and professional skills. Recent studies show that only 30% of Cambodian university students are enrolled in science and technology programs, while the remaining 70% focus on non-STEM fields, including languages, banking, finance, accounting, and hospitality. Meanwhile, the demand for professionals skilled in data analysis, data science, robotics, automation, AI, and machine learning grows every day.

At CamTech, we aim to change the traditional mindset that Cambodia must rely on foreign experts for specialized solutions. While we recognize the value of cultural exchange and shared expertise, our priority is to empower and elevate local talent to meet international standards. Our mission is to provide world-class education and training for learners at every stage, from university students to adults who want to acquire high-demand skills to business professionals looking to enhance their expertise. Through partnerships with universities worldwide and hands-on internships with leading local and regional companies, CamTech equips students with practical experience that often leads to career opportunities.

We are dedicated to supporting each student on their journey toward a meaningful career, whether in employment, entrepreneurship, or advanced studies. Together, let us build a future where Cambodian talent is recognized and celebrated on a global stage.
