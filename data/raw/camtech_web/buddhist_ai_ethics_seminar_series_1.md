Phnom Penh, November 8, 2023 – The Buddhist AI Ethics Seminar Series 1, hosted by CamTech University in collaboration with Kol Tor Teung Pagoda, brought together a distinguished panel of experts from various fields to critically discuss the significance of Buddhism’s core teachings in the modern era, particularly in the context of emerging technologies like Artificial Intelligence (AI). The seminar, attended by 150 participants from CamTech University, including leadership team members, faculty members, staff, and students, aimed to explore the intersection of Buddhism and technology.
