CamTech is pleased to announce that its President, Dr. Khieng Sothy, has recently launched his edited volume entitled “Cambodian Post-Secondary Education and Training in Global Knowledge Societies.” The project was supported by the Cambodia Development Resource Institute (CDRI). To read the book, please visit: https://lnkd.in/gJB3-EEP

Moreover, to further encourage academic and research activities and promote scholarly environment, CamTech has also inaugurated the first stage of its digital library with institutional subscription of various books and scientific journals including Nature, Science, and JSTOR etc. Such materials are available for free with unlimited access for the university’s staff, students, and visiting scholars.
