Dr. Seet Ai Mee former Minister of State for Education, Singapore (1988-1991) come to visit CamTech

On the morning of July 29, 2022, CamTech University welcomed Dr Seet Ai Mee, former Minister of State for Education, Singapore (1988-1991), currently the Founder and Chairman of E2STEM. Dr. Seet Ai Mee expressed her pleasure to visit CamTech.

In addition, Dr. Seet Ai Mee also met with former students of E2STEM School, who are students who have received full scholarships to study for 4 years at CamTech University. Dr. Seet Ai Mee revealed that all three of them are outstanding alumni of E2STEM. Currently, they are freshmen at CamTech university.

1. Mr. Line Regene, majoring in Cyber Security

2. Mr. Ung Chhoungchhav, majoring in Cyber Security

3. Mr. Ho Chanponleusophea, majoring in Robotics and Automation Engineering

Dr. Seet Ai Mee expressed a strong interest in the university, which has a good physical infrastructure and curriculum that responds to the 21st-century learning environment.
