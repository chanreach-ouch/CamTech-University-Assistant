CamTech University organized a counseling workshop on major selection and career orientation at Preah Monivong High School and Net Yang High School in Battambang province

Choosing a major that is in need in the labor market, looking for accommodation, and expenses are still major issues for students who pursue a university education. Some students have already chosen their major, while others have not been able to find a major that they really love and that responds to the job market. Seeing this problem, the team and students of CamTech University organized a counseling workshop on major selection and career orientation on July 27, 2022, at Preah Monivong High School and Net Yang High School in Battambang province. This workshop served as a key for the grade-12 students to make the decision to pursue higher education.
