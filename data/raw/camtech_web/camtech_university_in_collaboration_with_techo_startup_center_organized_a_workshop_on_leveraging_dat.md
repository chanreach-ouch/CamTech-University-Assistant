On July 20, 2023, CamTech University in collaboration with Techo Startup Center organized a workshop on “Leveraging Data as a Fuel for Your Business Decision”.

The workshop showcased prominent guest speakers including Mr. Chenda Sovisal, an esteemed Applied NLP Associate Researcher from Techo Startup Center, Mr. Greg Wolszczak, Reporting and Analytics Manager representing Solution BI Cambodia, Mr. Seang Oudom, ADA Cambodia’s Location Insights Manager, Mr. Ean Sokchomrern, Customer Analytics and Business Portfolio Manager affiliated with J Trust Bank, and Dr. Prum Sophea, Vice Dean of CamTech University’s Faculty of Engineering.

The workshop highlighted the significance of utilizing data-driven approaches for making informed business decisions. Our esteemed speakers drew upon their practical experiences and expertise to discuss market trends, obstacles, case studies, and the latest technologies employed in various industries. We are delighted to have participants from diverse sectors including students.
