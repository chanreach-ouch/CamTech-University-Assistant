Management team and delegates of the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation paid courtesy visit to CamTech University

Management team and delegates of the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation paid courtesy visit to CamTech University on January 31, 2023. CamTech University is pleased to welcome the management team and delegates of the Ministry of Industry, Science, Technology and Innovation to visit the University and meet with the CamTech management team, professors, staff, students on the afternoon of January 31, 2023. The university management team presented the curriculum, internship projects, as well as students and majors.
