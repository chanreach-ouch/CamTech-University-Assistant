Cultural Affair Consul and Delegates, Embassy of the People’s Republic of China paid a visit to CamTech university

On 10th June 2022, Mr. Lin Rui Hua, Cultural Affair Consul and Delegates, Embassy of the People’s Republic of China, paid a visit to CamTech university. In this meeting, the two parties discussed research collaboration and the exchange of human resources between CamTech university and universities in China. Besides, Mr. Hua also visited CamTech campus in order to know more about classroom preparation, atmosphere, and structure. Mr. Lin Rui Hua stated that he is satisfied with STEM education and environment of CamTech University and he also thanks CamTech for a warm welcome.
