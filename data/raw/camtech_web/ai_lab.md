*The AI Lab for Education at CamTech University is a pioneering initiative aimed at integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) into the educational landscape. Launched in December 2022 and funded by the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports along with the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD), this lab focuses on enhancing the quality of education through innovative AI technologies.*

*The lab provides a platform for scholars and entrepreneurs to exchange ideas and collaborate on AI projects. It aims to explore the AI landscape in Cambodia, examine the role of digital technology in society, and foster industry-university linkages. The lab also supports research and development efforts to improve education through technology.*
