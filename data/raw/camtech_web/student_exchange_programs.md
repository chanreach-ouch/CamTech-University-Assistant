## Welcome International Student!

### Dear international scholars and students!

"It is with great pleasure that I extend a warm welcome to each of you as you embark on your educational journey with Cambodia University of Technology and Science (CamTech) – a university founded with internationalization at the heart of its mission. As the Founding President, I am thrilled to see the CamTech community expanding both regionally and internationally, and I trust that the university will provide you with rich opportunities for learning, growth, and development that you are seeking. In just five years, CamTech has grown from a few dozen students to a vibrant community of over 500, offering key programs in science, technology, and innovation. The university combines academic rigor with real-world impact, preparing you not only for a career but for a meaningful life. The world is moving fast. Artificial intelligence, smart systems, robotics, energy innovation, and advanced technologies are shaping our collective future. At CamTech, we embrace these developments with curiosity, ethical responsibilities, and an interdisciplinary mindset. By joining CamTech, you are becoming part of not just a university but a dynamic and supportive community committed to innovation and positive change. I hope that your time at CamTech will provide moments for reflection and exploration, as well as academic rigor. In recent months, I have explored topics extending beyond technology alone—AI for peace, ethical frameworks for AI governance, Buddhist perspectives on technology, and digital literacy for youth and adults in developing countries. These reflections inform CamTech’s interdisciplinary approach to education, research, and innovation. If I were to summarize CamTech in three words, they would be Job, Life, and Planet. Our mission is simple yet ambitious: to cultivate graduates who can work with purpose, live with integrity, and contribute meaningfully to society and the planet. I encourage you to explore what CamTech has to offer. Visit our campus, speak with our students, and listen to their experiences. Engage with our faculty and staff, whose dedication is centered on helping students flourish intellectually, ethically, and personally – in body, mind, heart, and spirit. If you are ready for transformation, our vibrant and supportive community is here to help you realize your full potential. CamTech is your platform to dream big, learn deeply, and make a difference. "

**Prof. Dr. Sothy KHIENG**
