Khmer traditional games have been associated with the lives of the Cambodian people for a long time. In the past, our ancestors normally played these games in the autumn after the harvest season, especially during the traditional Khmer New Year celebration. The Khmer traditional games that were popularly played at that time were Bos Angkunh, Leak Kanseng, Sdach Jong, Teanh Prot, Krong Me Moan, etc. The aesthetic value of all these games is based on the ideals embedded in the games by Khmer ancestors to reflect the realities of life of the ancient Khmer people at that time, along with many folk legends that have been narrated from mouth to mouth.
