On Friday, January 28, Ms Song Khenglay, EVP and COO of Canadia Bank; Ms. Chheng Sophea, Head of Consumer Banking Division; Mr Sam Haung, Head of the HR Department visited, CamTech University to discuss with the CamTech’s team led by Dr. Khieng Sothy, President of CamTech University, on potential areas of collaboration between the two institutions. Canadia Bank and CamTech University had a fruitful discussion by identifying key areas of collaboration, such as internship and employment for students, short training courses and knowledge sharing sessions, joint research and consultancy projects, potential innovation projects such as those to promote cashless payment, property loan/student loan, and STEM Genius Scholarship. Canadia Bank and CamTech University shared the same vision and goals, reflecting Neak Oknha Dr. Pung Khiev Se’s commitment to promoting innovation to support the vision of Digital Economy and Society of Cambodia.
