CamTech University Football Team, CamTech United, has participated in the 2022 National Higher Education and Technical High School Friendly Student Sports Tournament

CamTech University Football Team, CamTech United, has participated in the 2022 National Higher Education and Technical High School Friendly Student Sports Tournament. The football matches went smoothly and in a friendly manner with matches against the football teams from the Royal University of Agriculture, Chamkar Dong, and the University of Management and Economics, Battambang.

CamTech University would like to thank the Ministry of Education, Youth, and Sports for organizing these national friendly football matches. A big thank to CamTech United for their hard work in their training as well as in the matches. Sport brings health, Sports brings brotherhood and peace.
