On Friday, January 28, the Chip Mong Group team, led by Dr. Im Sothearith, Director of the Human Resource Department, came to visit CamTech University’s campus to talk about potential areas of collaboration. CamTech University and the Chip Mong Group had a very fruitful and dynamic discussion as the two parties shared a similar vision and mission. 

CamTech University aims to be a leading university in the fields of science and technology to guarantee Cambodia’s economic development and future prosperity and to offer education that responds to the needs of industrial development and socio-economic growth.

Chip Mong Group is one of the well-established corporations in Cambodia that has contributed significantly to the economic growth of the country. Chip Mong Group’s core values include “Collaboration with Commitment”, “Making a Difference”, “Growing and Giving Back” and “Customer Focus” (CMGC). 

Chip Mong Group’s core values, particularly “Growing and Giving Back”, are well-aligned with CamTech’s objective of producing young talents in order to support the economic growth and welfare of Cambodia. Thus, CamTech University is pleased to announce a partnership with the Chip Mong Group to provide STEM Genius Scholarships, Internships, and Employment Opportunities for young potential Cambodian talents.

A Campus Visit from Her Excellency Koung Sorita,
Under-Secretary of State of Ministry of Women's Affairs,
and Mrs. Chea Sokheak,
CEO of Sorya Center Point

On Thursday, 23 December​ 2021, CamTech University was honored to receive a visit from Her Excellency Koung Sorita, Under-Secretary of State of Ministry of Women’s Affairs (MOWA), and Mrs. Chea Sokheak, CEO of Sorya Center Point. The campus visit is initiated with the purpose of introducing CamTech University to the Ministry of Women’s Affairs and exchanging ideas in order to explore possible areas of collaboration. CamTech University and MOWA acknowledged that current labor supplies have failed to catch up with the growing demand for skilled labor from the industries as Cambodia is transitioning to Digital Economy and Industry 4.0.

It is worth highlighting that CamTech’s goals and vision align well with those of MOWA since we both are striving to promote STEAM education, entrepreneurship, and woman empowerment. CamTech University is dedicated to building more partnerships with relevant Ministry, Industry and Community to provide high-quality education and equip our students with pertinent skills and knowledge.

MOU signing CamTech, Smart Axiata, and Impact Hub Phnom Penh

On Friday, December 17th, 2021, H.E. Dr. Khieng Sothy, representing CamTech University, signed a Tri-Party MOU with Smart Axiata, represented by Mr. Feiruz Ikhwan, acting CEO of Smart Axiata, and Impact Hub Phnom Penh (Cambodia) Co. Ltd., represented by Mrs. Melanie Mossard, CEO of Impact Hub Phnom Penh, regarding an online entrepreneur platform using the SmartStart Unipreneur Learning Platform Program. The signing event had honorable presents from H.E. Dr. Hang Chuon Narong, Minister of Education, and H.E. Vandeth Chea, Minister of Post and Telecommunications. CamTech is a private, non-profit, and research-incentive university among ten leading public and private universities that signed this MOU. Two of our senior lecturers had completed entrepreneurship training using the SmartStart Unipreneur Learning Platform prior to this event. CamTech will use the SmartStart Unipreneur Learning Platform as part of entrepreneurship courses as well as in our Social Innovation Center (SCI). 

The SmartStart Unipreneur Learning Platform is an educational initiative between Impact Hub Phnom Penh and Smart Axiata Co., Ltd. It is the first local online learning platform that introduces entrepreneurship through a blended learning approach to Cambodian students in Khmer and English. Its main goal is to develop technical capabilities in entrepreneurship and business and to foster entrepreneurial mindsets through the practical implementation of these concepts.
