CamTech Cultural Day

Khmer traditional games have been associated with the lives of the Cambodian people for a long time. In the past, our ancestors normally played these games in the autumn after the harvest season, especially during the traditional Khmer New Year celebration. The Khmer traditional games that were popularly played at that time were Bos Angkunh, Leak Kanseng, Sdach Jong, Teanh Prot, Krong Me Moan, etc. The aesthetic value of all these games is based on the ideals embedded in the games by Khmer ancestors to reflect the realities of life of the ancient Khmer people at that time, along with many folk legends that have been narrated from mouth to mouth.

A Life in Science

On Monday, 21st March 2022, the students of CamTech University were given a captivating and essential special lecture by Professor Chhem Kieth Rethy on the topic “A Life in Science”.

Explaining the differences between nature, nurture, and chance has enlightened young people as they realize that humans are all born with unique abilities. As a result, it is vitally important to maintain our self-confidence and positive attitudes to keep moving forward in the face of challenges.

Professor Rethy’s speech about his life journey to becoming a scientist has inspired all the CamTech students to pursue their dreams and do what they believe is right for themselves. “I have no regrets in my life because I do what I want to do,” the professor responded to one of the students’ inquiry. These remarks have enabled the students to perceive their own potential.

As long as you hold an unwavering passion or potential for a particular endeavor, nothing is impossible in life.

Introduction to Humanities - ETHICS FOR ENGINEERING

Don’t Forget The Lives of Which You Are Responsible!

[ETHICS FOR ENGINEERING]

On Monday the 14th of March 2022, the students of CamTech University were given a captivating and essential seminar by Professor Rethy K. Chhem on the Ethics of Engineering and the disasters that may happen in the absence of said Ethics.

Showcasing various shocking engineering tragedies that have plagued us, the Professor explains the calamities as results of Engineering Failures caused by a lack of ethical understanding and practice.

Professor Rethy encourages the students of CamTech not to forget the application of Humanism and Morals in their creations, which will only inevitably make our designs more successful, and we ourselves, better people.

As the Professor had quoted in this special seminar

“The Strength of a Nation derives from the Integrity of the Home” -Confucius

We can only be great as a whole, if we, as well, are great as individuals.
