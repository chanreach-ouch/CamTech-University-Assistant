CamTech University Strengthens Cambodia's Research and Innovation Ecosystem through Cambodia Research Network

CamTech University has reaffirmed its commitment to advancing Cambodia’s research capability and innovation ecosystem by becoming a founding member of the Cambodia Research Network (CRN), a national consortium that brings together 31 leading research-oriented universities and research institutions to strengthen evidence-based research, policy engagement, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

The University’s participation reflects its long-term strategy to cultivate a vibrant research culture, develop the next generation of researchers and innovators, and translate scientific knowledge into practical solutions that address Cambodia’s development priorities. As Cambodia’s leading technology-focused university, CamTech views research not only as an academic pursuit but also as a driver of innovation, industry collaboration, and national competitiveness.

The Cambodia Research Network was formally launched on 22 July 2026 through a Memorandum of Understanding signed between the Economic, Social and Cultural Council (EcoSoc) under the Office of the Council of Ministers and 31 research-oriented universities and research institutions. The signing ceremony was presided over by His Excellency Vongsey Vissoth, Permanent Deputy Prime Minister and Minister in Charge of the Office of the Council of Ministers.

During the ceremony, His Excellency underscored the profound impact of global megatrends and artificial intelligence on Cambodia’s future, stressing that evidence-based research is essential for building national resilience, informing public policy, and supporting the implementation of the Royal Government’s Pentagonal Strategy. He described the Cambodia Research Network as the beginning of a new chapter in mobilising Cambodia’s intellectual community to contribute to national development.

CamTech has invested strategically in building research capacity through the establishment of its international graduate programme in Science, Technology and Innovation. The University also hosts the Comparative Education Society of Cambodia (CESCam), the Centre for Buddhist AI Ethics, the China–ASEAN Studies Centre, and the AI Forum, which foster interdisciplinary research and dialogue on emerging issues of national, regional, and global significance.

Complementing these initiatives, CamTech University Press promotes the dissemination of scholarly research and facilitates knowledge exchange among academics, industry, policymakers, and international partners. Together, these initiatives strengthen Cambodia’s research capability while advancing applied research, technological innovation, and evidence-based solutions.

Through its participation in the Cambodia Research Network, CamTech University looks forward to expanding research collaboration with government, academia, industry, and international partners, contributing expertise that accelerates innovation and supports Cambodia’s sustainable socio-economic development.

CamTech and E-GetS Sign MOU on Strategic Partnership

On the morning of November 19, the signing ceremony for a memorandum of understanding (hereinafter MOU) between Cambodia University of Technology and Science and E-GetS was held in Phnom Penh. CamTech President Prof. Dr. Khieng Sothy, Associate Vice President for Business Development Ms. Lilian Li, E-GetS Vice Presidents Mr. Zhang Juhui and Ms. Wang Huifang, student and teacher representatives, as well as local and international media attended the event. The ceremony marks the prologue of collaboration to advance the integration of higher education and the digital technology industry in Cambodia.
The workshop was held on the afternoon of June 30, 2022, with the participation of 44 teachers and students. In this workshop, CamTech students also shared their learning experiences and presented various projects such as AR / VR Games to the students at Sovannaphumi School.
During the event, Ms. Lilian Li outlined key initiatives under the MOU. As part of the collaboration, CamTech and E-GetS will jointly establish an employment training and internship base, designed to bridge academic knowledge with cutting-edge industry practice. Furthermore, E-GetS will offer USD 100,000 to CamTech’s scholarship fund for talent development and targeted training. Ms. Li praised E-GetS’s support and highlighted that these efforts will equip students with more profound professional experience, fostering new talent for Cambodia’s digital transformation.
 

In his address, Mr. Zhang Juhui gave a detailed introduction to E-GetS’s development history, business scope, and corporate culture. He emphasized that the company will leverage its technological and platform strengths to collaborate with CamTech in operating the training base, offering students practical opportunities, scholarships, and specialized training programs. The partnership aims to cultivate versatile talent for the digital economy and drive Cambodia’s digital transformation.

Following the speeches, senior representatives from CamTech and E-GetS signed the MOU and officially established their partnership. As a symbol of the cooperation’s transition into action, CamTech presented E-GetS the honorary “Employment Training and Internship Base” plaque, while E-GetS provided a USD 100,000 scholarship for the university.

The collaboration between CamTech and E-GetS demonstrates a strong synergy between industry and academia. Through complementary strengths, the two parties strive to promote talent cultivation and technology innovation, driving integration and development across the education and technology sectors.

CamTech University and Touch Sonn Foundation Forge Historic Partnership in Shaping Cambodia's Future

Phnom Penh, Cambodia – August 17, 2024 – CamTech University, a pioneering institution in technology and science, today celebrated a significant milestone with the signing of a landmark funding agreement between the Touch Sonn Foundation and the University of Technology and Science of Cambodia (CamTech).

The program aims to empower talented students by providing opportunities for tertiary and postgraduate education, while also equipping them with cutting-edge skills in STEM majors. This strategic initiative aligns with the future market demand, enabling students to contribute to Cambodia’s economic growth and development.
Touch Samnang, Representative of the Touch Sonn Foundation’s family, shared the foundation’s vision: “Our late founder, Father Touch Sonn, believed that education is the greatest inheritance. He instilled in us the importance of empowering individuals and communities through quality education. Through this foundation, we aim to continue his legacy by providing access to quality education for students.”

The Touch Sonn Foundation has donated a substantial sum of US $300,000 to CamTech University to provide loans to students at low-interest rates. This marks the first major donation from the foundation to CamTech’s Endowment Fund, which was established to support student financial aid, academic programs, and infrastructure development.

Prof. Dr. Khieng Sothy, Founding President of CamTech University, expressed gratitude for the foundation’s support, stating that the funds received will enable the university to become more financially sustainable and increase enrollment rates, particularly among female students and those from remote areas.

CamTech University was established with a vision to become a leading institution in technology and science, and its mission is to provide high-quality education and promote academic excellence through innovation and entrepreneurship.

CamTech University welcomes all generous individuals to join this journey of shaping the future of education in Cambodia. We believe that every contribution, no matter how small, can make a significant difference in the lives of our students. Together, we can create a brighter future for our country.

The President of CamTech University and delegates from CamTech recently took part in the inauguration of the League of Tropical Universities.
Dr. Sothy Khieng, the president of CamTech University, along with CamTech delegates, recently participated in the Inauguration of the League of Tropical Universities, which took place from November 13-15, 2023. 
The primary objective of the League is to collectively address the major challenges faced by tropical regions and enhance the global collaborations between tropical universities, research institutions, government bodies, industries, and international organizations. The League focuses on various key areas including, but not limited to, the advancement of technology, agricultural innovations, social cooperation, and climate science. By fostering a community-based approach and prioritizing collaboration in crucial domains, the League serves as a unique platform that facilitates resource sharing, talent development, joint research initiatives, technology transfer, and innovation among its member institutions.
