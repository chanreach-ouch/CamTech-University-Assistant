Prof Dr Sothy Khieng Invited to Present at 11th Nishan Forum on World Civilizations in Qufu, China

Prof Dr Sothy Khieng was invited to present at the 11th Nishan Forum on World Civilizations in Qufu, China – the birthplace of Confucius. 🏯

He proposed reframing AI ethics through Buddhist and Eastern moral traditions, advocating for “Humane AI” that actively reduces suffering (dukkha) (ទុក្ខ)​. Key insights:

🔹 Compassion (Karunā) (ករុណា)​ Efficiency: Embed empathy in algorithms, especially for healthcare, education, and social welfare.
🔹 Ahimsa in Tech (អហិង្សា)​: Design AI that avoids harm to people, societies, and ecosystems.
🔹 Confucian-Buddhist Synergy: Combine “rén” (benevolence) and mindfulness to prioritize collective well-being over profit-driven systems.

In this age of algorithmic bias and existential anxiety, Eastern ethics offer timeless tools: intention-aware design, karmic responsibility, and the Middle Path between utopia and doom.

Grateful to CamTech’s Center for Buddhist AI Ethics and fellow scholars for pioneering this dialogue.

Meeting with the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS)

On March 14, 2025, the Future Skills Mission to Cambodia, led by H.E. San Vathana, Undersecretary of State at the Ministry of Education, Youth, and Sports, brought together leaders from public and private universities to meet with the Australian Embassy in Phnom Penh and Austrade representatives.

The discussion aims to foster institutional partnerships, support Cambodia’s long-term education strategy, and explore innovative learning solutions for the future.
CamTech University is honored to be part of this significant discussion, represented by Prof. Dr. Sothy Khieng, Founding President, and Prof. Dr. Leng Phirom, Vice President for Academic Affairs.

Science Diplomacy in a Changing World

CamTech University, in collaboration with the National Institute of Science, Technology and Innovation (NISTI) and the AI Forum, co-organized the workshop “Science Diplomacy in a Changing World” on 29 April 2025

This important event brought together diplomats, academics, scientists, and industry leaders to explore how science diplomacy can advance international cooperation, respond to global challenges, and ensure inclusive access to emerging technologies. Discussions emphasized the vital role of linking research to policy and fostering cross-border scientific collaboration to drive innovation, inform decision-making, and promote sustainable development.

CamTech Signs MoU with St. John's College to Foster Academic Collaboration and Exchange

November 21, 2024 – CamTech is excited to announce that its delegates recently held a productive meeting with St. John’s College, located in Annapolis, Maryland, which is over 300 years old and recognized as the third oldest college in the United States.

The college is known for its distinctive great books curriculum and its commitment to liberal arts education, which emphasizes critical thinking, rigorous dialogue, and a deep exploration of foundational texts in Western civilization.

During this meeting, the two institutions signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to establish a collaborative partnership aimed at enhancing academic cooperation. This partnership will include initiatives such as:

– Faculty and Student Exchange Programs
– Joint Academic Initiatives
– 3+1 Joint Degree Programs for undergraduate and graduate studies

The University Presidents Meeting East Asia & Pacific 2024
CamTech is proud to be part of the Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN), the world’s largest knowledge network for the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). On May 15th, CamTech’s management team participated in the 2024 UNSDSN University President Meeting, chaired by Professor Jeffrey Sachs. This virtual high-level meeting brought together University Leaders, representatives from the UN and Governments, along with sustainability experts from over 100 organizations across Asia and beyond. The meeting focused on the crucial role of higher education in promoting sustainable development.
